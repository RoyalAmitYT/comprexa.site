FAQ

Will extracted pages retain full document quality?

Yes! Comprexa copies vector structures, embedded fonts, and images natively without re-compressing them. Original quality is preserved 100% losslessly.

Can I select pages using custom page numbers or ranges?

Yes! You can either click individual thumbnails or type custom page ranges like "1-4, 7, 10-12" into the range input box.
